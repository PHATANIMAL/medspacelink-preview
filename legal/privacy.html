Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. This policy explains what the MedSpaceLink iOS app and medspacelink.com ("MedSpaceLink", "we") collect and why. MedSpaceLink is a free marketplace where licensed clinicians find and share medical office space (exam rooms, consult offices, operatories) by the day, half-day or month.

The short version

  • MedSpaceLink is for renting rooms, not for patient care. We do not collect, store or process patient health information (PHI), and you must not enter any.
  • The iOS app keeps your profile, saved rooms, requests and listing drafts on your phone only. They are not sent to us.
  • We do not sell personal data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Payments for room bookings on medspacelink.com are processed by Stripe and go directly to the host. We never see your full card number.

Data stored only on your device (iOS app)

When you use the app, the following stays in the app’s local storage on your phone and is not transmitted to MedSpaceLink:

  • Profile you type in (name, email, role: clinician or host).
  • Onboarding choices (specialty, borough), saved rooms, appearance setting.
  • Room requests and listing drafts you create in the app.
  • Listings you report or hide.

You can erase all of it at any time in Profile → Delete my data. Deleting the app also erases it.

Location

If you tap “Locate me” on the map, the app asks iOS for your location while the app is in use. Your location is used on your device only, to center the map. It is not stored or sent to us. You can decline or change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Information collected on medspacelink.com

If you create an account, list a space, book, or donate on medspacelink.com, we collect what you give us to provide that service:

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), role, and optionally practice name, specialty and phone number.
  • Listing details hosts publish: address or neighborhood, photos, rates, availability, amenities and description. Published listings are public.
  • Profile photo and specialty, if you choose to add them.
  • Device push-notification token, only if you turn on notifications in a MedSpaceLink app that offers them (this iOS app does not).
  • Booking details: the room, dates, and messages between clinician and host about the booking.
  • How you heard about us (optional) and the campaign tags on the link you arrived from, to understand which outreach works.
  • Basic server logs and usage information (IP address, browser type, pages requested, searches) kept for security, troubleshooting and improving the service. The website uses session cookies to keep you signed in, and no advertising or tracking cookies.

Payments (Stripe)

Room bookings are paid by card through Stripe. Hosts are paid through Stripe Connect accounts in their own name, and Stripe’s processing fee is deducted from the host’s payout. MedSpaceLink charges no platform fee or commission. Card details are entered on Stripe-hosted fields and handled by Stripe under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). Hosts provide identity and bank details directly to Stripe to receive payouts. We receive limited payment records (amount, status, last 4 digits, Stripe IDs) to show booking status. Optional donations to support MedSpaceLink are made on the website, also through Stripe; the iOS app does not take donations.

Third-party services

  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap (or MapTiler, if enabled). When you view the map, your device requests map images for the area on screen, which reveals your IP address to the tile provider.
  • Live listings: the app may load public listings from medspacelink.com. These requests carry no personal data.
  • Stripe for payments (website only). Resend to send account and booking emails. Replit for hosting the website and its database.

How we use information

  • Run the marketplace: show listings, connect clinicians and hosts, process bookings.
  • Send service emails about your account or bookings.
  • Keep the service safe: prevent fraud, review reported listings, enforce our Terms.
  • Count signups and which outreach brought them, in aggregate.

Sharing

We share information only: with the other party to a booking (for example, your name and requested dates go to the host); with Stripe to process payments; with service providers that host our website; or when required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Retention and deletion

Website account data is kept while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your medspacelink.com account and its personal data at any time by writing to [[SUPPORT EMAIL – Phat to provide]]; we act on requests within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons (for example Stripe payment records). In the iOS app, Profile → Delete my data erases everything the app stored, immediately.

Children

MedSpaceLink is for healthcare professionals and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to certain uses. Contact [[SUPPORT EMAIL – Phat to provide]] and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, hash passwords with bcrypt, and restrict who can access production data. No system is perfectly secure; please use a unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell account holders by email or in the app.
