Common questions

  • Is it free? Yes. Signing up, listing and requesting are free. MedSpaceLink charges hosts and clinicians nothing. Card bookings on the website carry Stripe’s processing fee, which is deducted from the host’s payout.
  • What are “Demo listing” rooms? Fictional examples that show how the app works. They are not real offices.
  • Where is my data? In the app, your profile, saved rooms, requests and drafts are stored on your phone only.
  • How do I delete my data? In the app: Profile → Delete my data. For a medspacelink.com account, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • How do I report a listing? Open the listing, tap ⋯ (More) → Report listing. You can also hide the listing or everything from that host.
  • Why does the map ask for my location? Only to center the map on you when you tap “Locate me”. Your location stays on your phone.
